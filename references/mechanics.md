# Punctuation and formatting

## Em dashes

Avoid them. Use periods or commas. Do not substitute parentheses, en dashes, or hyphens-as-dashes; that trades one tell for another. If a thought needs separation, end the sentence or use a comma.

## Colons

Colons are fine before a list, a label, or an example. They are not mid-sentence connectors.

Weak: "If you're coming from traditional automation: instead of registering event handlers, you describe conditions."

The colon adds nothing. Rewrite so the point stands alone: "Describing when the scheduler should fire works best as plain English."

## Boldface

Do not bold every proper noun or acronym. Bold only where the reader would lose the thread without it.

## Inline-header lists

The tell is a bold label and colon that restates the line:

> **Performance:** Performance improved by 30%.

Convert those to prose.

A bold lead-in that ends in a period, names the item, and is followed by new detail is fine:

> **Schema in TypeScript.** Tables live in one file.

That is not a tell.

Also avoid converting connected prose into a vertical list. Fewer than four items, or items that logically flow, belong in a paragraph.

## Headings

Use sentence case. Title Case On Every Main Word is an AI habit. Capitalize the first word and proper nouns.

## Emoji

Remove decorative emoji from headings and bullets unless the genre demands them.

## Quotation marks

Use straight quotes. Pick one style and hold it; mixing curly and straight marks in one document is a chatbot artifact.

## Markdown and chatbot debris

Delete silently when rewriting:

- Horizontal rules (`---`) inserted before every heading
- Code fences wrapped around prose
- Tracking parameters left in links (`utm_source=chatgpt.com`)
- Citation fragments (`turn0search0`, `oaicite`)
- Placeholder text (`[Your Name]`, `[Insert date]`)

## Summary sections

Delete a closing section that restates what the document already said. If a long document needs an ending, the ending contains something new: a decision, a next step, or a consequence.
